Webmin upstream licensing (reference only)

Files under compat/webmin/ are an unmodified copy of upstream Webmin
(https://github.com/webmin/webmin), pinned in compat/webmin.lock. They are not
relicensed by Homelab Control and are not included in any Homelab Control
release package.

Webmin as a whole is distributed under the BSD 3-Clause licence; its full text
is in compat/webmin/LICENCE. Some individual files state a different licence in
their own headers (for example GPL-2.0-or-later, GPL or LGPL); for those files
the licence stated in the file applies. Third-party JavaScript libraries inside
the tree carry their own notices.

See docs/compliance/LICENSE-AUDIT.md (finding LA-08) for the files identified
during the Homelab Control licence audit.
